Distribution notes
------------------
Tauri bundles ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe through bundle.externalBin and copies
this ffmpeg resource directory, including all seven required DLLs, into every
future bundle. The standard configuration and the dedicated offline overlay
therefore inherit the same sidecars and resources. Development uses the
target-suffixed executables directly from src-tauri/binaries and these same
DLLs. End users do not need FFmpeg installed.

LGPL compliance requires preserving notices and the license, allowing library
replacement/debugging, and making the exact corresponding source available.
Before publishing an installer, host the corresponding source archive alongside
the FileNova download and retain the source/build URLs above. Codec patent
licensing is a separate commercial-distribution consideration.
